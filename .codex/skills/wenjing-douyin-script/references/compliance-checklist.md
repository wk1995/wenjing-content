# 抖音脚本合规检查

Use this reference before finalizing commerce, beauty, health, finance, education, and ad-delivery scripts.

## Must Remove

- 绝对化：最好、第一、唯一、顶级、100%、全网最低、永久、彻底。
- 未证实功效：治好、根治、保证、马上见效、7 天游效、28 天淡斑 83%。
- 医疗化表达：消炎、杀菌、治疗、修复屏障 if the product cannot legally claim it.
- 贬损竞品：吊打、秒杀所有竞品、比某品牌强太多。
- 虚假紧迫：不买就亏、错过再等一年 unless the campaign fact supports it.

## Safer Rewrite Patterns

- "60 秒缓解敏感" -> "用完感觉舒服多了"
- "28 天淡斑 83%" -> "坚持用一阵子，肤色看起来更匀"
- "治好痘痘" -> "帮助改善痘痘肌状态"
- "全网最低" -> "这次价格很适合入手"
- "医院要花几千" -> avoid implying medical replacement

## Review Checklist

Before final answer:

1. Mark any high-risk phrase.
2. Replace risky efficacy claims with first-person experience or softer sensory language.
3. Keep proof proportional to provided evidence.
4. Separate creative hook from factual claim.
5. Mention uncertainty when the product information is incomplete.
